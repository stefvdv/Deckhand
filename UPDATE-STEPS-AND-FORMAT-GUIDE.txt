ADMIRAL v2.31 — UPDATE STEPS AND FORMAT UPDATE GUIDE
=====================================================================

PART 1 — DEPLOYING AN UPDATE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Netlify drag-and-drop REPLACES the whole site, so ALWAYS deploy this
COMPLETE folder (9 files):

  index.html, sw.js, manifest.json, format.txt,
  icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable-512.png,
  apple-touch-icon.png  (+ this guide)

Steps:
  1. Netlify -> your site -> Deploys
  2. Drag this whole folder onto the page
  3. Open the site on your phone; Extras should show the new version

Players' installed apps update themselves on their next open —
nothing for them to do.


PART 2 — UPDATING THE FORMAT WITH format.txt
---------------------------------------------------------------------
format.txt is your control panel. Editing it changes the format for
every player, with zero code changes. Any text editor works, even on
a phone.

The file has four sections:

  [banned]       The COMPLETE banlist. One card name per line.
  [ub-sets]      The curated Universes Beyond sets shown behind
                 the UB slider. One official set name per line.
  [quick-rules]  The short rules summary players see first under
                 Extras -> Format Rules. One bullet per line.
  [full-rules]   The complete rules behind "Read the full rules".
                 One paragraph per line.

--- To BAN a card ---
  1. Open format.txt
  2. Under [banned], add one line with the card's exact English
     name, e.g.:
         Nexus of Fate
  3. Save, then deploy the WHOLE folder (Part 1)

--- To UNBAN a card ---
  1. Find its line under [banned] and delete the entire line
  2. Save and deploy the whole folder

--- To ADD or REMOVE a Universes Beyond set ---
  1. Under [ub-sets], add or delete a line with the set's official
     name, e.g.:
         Final Fantasy
  2. Save and deploy the whole folder

--- Getting card names right ---
  Spelling must match the card exactly, including commas and
  titles: "Azusa, Lost but Seeking", not "azusa".
  Easiest method: search the card in the app (or scryfall.com)
  and copy the name as shown.

--- Verifying your change ---
  After deploying, open the app:
  * You'll get a "The format has changed" popup listing exactly
    what you added or removed (players get the same popup).
  * Open Extras -> Banned List and TAP your new entry — if the
    card's image appears, the spelling is correct and the ban is
    airtight. If it says not found, fix the line and redeploy.

--- To EDIT the format rules ---
  Both rules sections are plain text with three simple markers:

     == Title       starts a new section header
     > text         renders as a smaller, muted example line
     **words**      makes those words bold
     anything else  is a normal paragraph (one per line)

  1. Find the sentence you want to change under [quick-rules]
     or [full-rules] and edit it in place -- or add a new line
     where you want a new bullet, paragraph, header (==) or
     example (>)
  2. Keep each paragraph on ONE line (let your editor wrap it
     visually; do not press Enter mid-paragraph)
  3. Save and deploy the whole folder
  4. Every player gets a popup: "The format rules were updated"
     -- and Extras -> Format Rules shows your new text, styled
     exactly like before

  Tip: mana costs like {2} can be typed as-is. Avoid < and >
  at the start of a line unless you mean an example line.

--- Safety net ---
  If format.txt is missing or broken, the app silently falls back
  to its built-in list. A typo can never break the app.

--- What needs NO action at all ---
  New Standard sets legalize themselves within a day of release
  (players get a popup announcing them). Universes Beyond sets
  never auto-join — only your [ub-sets] list applies.
